## Important: production-only cost (per your note #16)
For the show we use **production cost only — materials + labour + overhead**. We **leave out**
your domestic selling costs (Pitch 8%, local bank 2.875%) because they don't apply in Dubai.
Instead the show has:
- **VAT 5%** — the **customer pays** this on top, so it's not your cost.
- **Card charge 3%** — **you absorb** it on card sales (cash has none). It's in the model, scaled
  by the share of sales paid by card.

So send me your **stock list** and I'll produce a **show costing** for those pieces (your normal
cost cards with the selling-% section removed) to get the true average production cost.
