# Vol 2 — music for the video posts

A shortlist of audio for the 10 outfit videos (the center post in each grid trio). Goal: a
**cohesive, luxury feel** across the drop, not ten random songs. **Pick from Instagram's own
audio library so the Reel gets the trending-audio reach boost** — the titles below are the
*vibe* to search for, plus a fallback if a track isn't licensed for business accounts.

## First rule: use trending audio when you can
Reels attached to an audio that's trending get pushed harder. Before scheduling:
1. Open Instagram → **Reels → search the audio name** below → tap it → look for the **↗ arrow
   next to the title = trending**.
2. Business accounts only see *commercially-licensed* audio. If a song is greyed out, use the
   **fallback instrumental** row — no reach penalty, just less viral lift.
3. **Save 8–10 audios to your "Saved audio"** (tap the bookmark on the audio page) so the whole
   team can grab the same set — keeps the drop consistent.

## The drop's sound: one lane
Keep all 10 in the **same family** so the grid feels like one collection:
**slow-to-mid luxury** — sophisticated, feminine, a little cinematic. Two acceptable moods:
- **A) Soft & elegant** (most pieces) — dreamy, romantic, unhurried.
- **B) Bold & confident** (the statement colours) — a slightly heavier beat, still classy.

## Teaser + drop-day audio (16–19 July)
For the **teasers and the launch announcement**, pick **one signature track** and reuse it on
all teaser clips — repetition makes people *recognise* the drop. Choose a slightly bigger,
trending one here (a current viral Coke Studio / Bollywood slowed track) since these are your
reach posts.

## Quick rules for the team
- **Match the cut to the beat** — align the outfit's spin/walk to a drop or a lyric hit.
- **Keep vocals low if there's a voiceover;** otherwise let the audio carry it.
- **Same 2–3 audios can repeat across outfits** — a signature sound is good, not lazy.
- **Check the audio isn't tied to another brand's viral moment** that clashes with luxury.
- Instagram trends shift weekly — **re-check the ↗ trending arrow the day you schedule**, don't
  rely on a track being hot two weeks later.

*Audio choices are creative — this is a starting shortlist. Swap freely for whatever's trending
and on-brand the week of the drop.*
