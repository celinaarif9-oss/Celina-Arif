# Vol 2 — Scheduling walkthrough (Meta Business Suite)

Queue the whole launch week in **one ~30-minute sitting**. Free, schedules **Instagram +
Facebook together**. General how-to is in `social-scheduling.md`; this is the exact Vol 2 plan.

> **Approval:** you (or whoever you delegate) approve every caption and hit schedule. Nothing
> auto-posts. Pick your images first — creative stays with you.

## Before you start
- Open **business.facebook.com → Planner** (desktop) or the **Meta Business Suite** app → **Planner**.
- Have your **hero + detail images** ready for each day (see "Image" column).
- All times **8pm PKT** (peak PK evening; also catches UK daytime).

## The week — 6 scheduled posts
Create each as **Planner → Create post → Instagram + Facebook → Schedule** for the date/time.

| # | Date & time | Post | Image | Caption to paste |
|---|-------------|------|-------|------------------|
| 1 | **Wed 16 Jul, 8pm** | Teaser 1 (feed + Story) | Mood / fabric hint, no reveal | `Something new, this July.` |
| 2 | **Thu 17 Jul, 8pm** | Teaser 2 (feed + Story) | Behind-the-scenes / fabric close-up | `In the atelier this week.` |
| 3 | **Fri 18 Jul, 8pm** | Countdown (feed + Story) | Cropped tease of a hero piece | `Tomorrow. Vol 2 — live at 8pm.` |
| 4 | **Sat 19 Jul, 8pm** | **Launch** (feed + Story) | Collection hero | `Vol 2 is here. Made to order, in your size.` |
| 5 | **Sun 20 Jul** | #wearC repost (Story, manual) | First customer photo | `[Name] in [piece]. #wearC` |
| 6 | **Tue 22 Jul, 8pm** | Sustain (feed + Story) | Best-seller detail shot | `Still available, made to order.` |

## Step-by-step for one post (repeat for each)
1. **Planner → Create post.**
2. **Add photo/video** → upload the image.
3. Paste the **caption** from the table (tweak if you like).
4. Tick **both Instagram and Facebook**.
5. Click **Schedule** → set the **date and 8:00pm** → confirm.
6. It now shows in the Planner calendar. Repeat for the next row.

*Stories:* in Planner, **Create story → Schedule** works the same way for #1–4 and #6. The #wearC
repost (#5) is easiest posted live from your phone when the first customer photo comes in.

## Emails (separate — Shopify, not Business Suite)
- **Fri 18 Jul:** send the **countdown email** (`Something new is coming`).
- **Sat 19 Jul, 8pm:** send the **launch email** (`Vol 2 is here`) — see `vol2-launch-plan.md`.

## After launch — drip the individual pieces
Over the days following the 19th, post pieces one at a time using the **per-piece captions** in
`vol2-launch-plan.md` (e.g. `Say hello to Serena. Velvet, in midnight blue.`). One piece per
post keeps the feed clean and gives each design its moment.

## Launch-day reminder
Before the 8pm launch post goes out, run the **5-minute go-live checklist** in
`vol2-launch-plan.md` (store live, prices/sizes/shipping working, test checkout).
