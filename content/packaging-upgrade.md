## 2. Make it feel luxury (tiered by budget)
Your AOV is Rs50k — the unboxing should match a couture purchase. Layers, in priority order:

**Tier 1 — biggest impact, modest cost**
- **Rigid box** (magnetic-close or lid-and-base) in a signature colour, with the logo
  **foil-stamped or embossed** (not printed). This one change is what separates "nice" from
  "luxury." ~Rs250–600/box at volume.
- **Branded tissue paper** wrapping the outfit, closed with a **logo sticker or wax seal**.
  Cheap, huge perceived-value lift.
- **Ribbon** in a brand colour around the tissue.

**Tier 2 — the couture signal**
- **Cloth dust bag / drawstring pouch** (cotton or non-woven) with the logo — reusable, keeps the
  outfit safe, and customers keep it. Very "made-to-order."
- **Butter/duplex garment folder or belly-band** around the folded outfit.

**Tier 3 — the personal, low-cost touches**
- **Handwritten name** on the thank-you card ("Dear [name],") — you already capture the name on
  the order tag. Feels bespoke; costs nothing.
- **QR code on the care card** linking to a 20-second "how to care for your outfit" video or your
  new arrivals — turns a throwaway card into a marketing touch.
- **A small fabric swatch** of the outfit tucked in (useful for future matching) — a signature
  detail almost no one does.

**Order tag upgrade:** rename the customer-facing fields so it reads like a luxury garment tag,
not a warehouse slip — e.g. a clean front with just the logo + "Made to order for you", and the
order no / article / size / price on the back. Keep the internal one separate.

## 4. "Come pick up and pack the order for me"
Two different needs — decide which you mean:

- **If you want the packaging delivered** so your own team packs at home → the suppliers above do
  exactly that (order online/WhatsApp, it arrives).
- **If you want someone to physically collect finished orders and pack + dispatch them** (a
  fulfillment / 3PL service) → for **made-to-order luxury with customisation, in-house packing is
  usually better** (quality control, the personal touches above). What you more likely want is
  **doorstep courier pickup** so you don't run to a courier office: **Leopards, TCS, and M&P**
  all offer scheduled home pickup, and the **courier aggregator apps** we set up in Phase 2
  (e.g. PostEx / Trax / the Shopify courier app) book a rider to collect from your address
  automatically once an order is packed. That gets you "packed at home → rider collects → gone"
  without handing packing to a third party.

If you genuinely want a third party to pack *and* ship (not just collect), tell me and I'll
shortlist Karachi fulfillment houses — but I'd flag it risks the bespoke feel that justifies your
price.
